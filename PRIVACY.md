# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 June 2026_

SmartSort is built to keep your data on your Mac. This policy explains what that means in practice.

## The short version

SmartSort does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no servers, no account system, and no analytics. Everything it does happens locally on your device.

## What SmartSort accesses

- **Folders and files you choose.** When you select a folder to organize, SmartSort reads the files in that folder so it can propose and apply a sort plan. This access stays on your device and is governed by the macOS App Sandbox — SmartSort can only touch folders you explicitly grant it.
- **On-device processing.** Categorization and duplicate detection run entirely on your Mac using the machine-learning frameworks built into macOS. File names and contents are never sent off the device.

## What SmartSort does not do

- It does **not** upload your files or their contents anywhere.
- It does **not** collect usage analytics, telemetry, or crash data.
- It does **not** require an account, email address, or sign-in.
- It does **not** use third-party SDKs, advertising, or trackers.

## Data sharing

SmartSort shares no data with the developer or any third party, because it collects none.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised date.
